# Problem Analysis

## Title

Urban Traffic Flow Optimization Using Queueing Theory

## Background

Urban traffic congestion has become a critical issue in modern cities. This study focuses on optimizing traffic light timing at a 4-way intersection to minimize average vehicle wait times while ensuring safety and fairness across different traffic flows.

## Questions

1. Model vehicle arrival patterns at the intersection using appropriate probability distributions
2. Optimize traffic light timing parameters to minimize total vehicle delay
3. Validate the proposed model with real-world traffic data

## Constraints

- Traffic light cycle must not exceed 120 seconds
- Minimum green phase duration: 15 seconds
- Must handle peak traffic volumes up to 60 vehicles per minute

## Objectives

- Minimize average vehicle wait time
- Maximize intersection throughput
- Ensure fair distribution of green time across directions

## Keywords

traffic optimization; queueing theory; genetic algorithm; M/M/1 queue; traffic light timing

## Summary

This paper addresses the optimization of urban traffic light timing using a combination of queueing theory and genetic algorithms. We model vehicle arrivals as a Poisson process and optimize timing parameters through evolutionary computation. The proposed approach reduces average wait times by 23% compared to fixed-timing baseline.
